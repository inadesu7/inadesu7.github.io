## Why Give to INADESU?

✓ **Direct impact** – Funds support on-the-ground programs in real communities  
✓ **Transparent** – We share detailed reports on how your donation is used  
✓ **Accountable** – As a registered NGO, we maintain strict financial oversight  
✓ **Efficient** – A significant portion of every donation reaches our beneficiaries  

---

## How Your Donation Helps

**$10** – Provides school supplies for one child  
**$25** – Funds a health awareness session  
**$50** – Supports skills training for one person  
**$100** – Provides emergency assistance to a vulnerable family  
**$250+** – Enables comprehensive program support
