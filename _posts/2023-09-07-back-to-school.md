## Supporting children going back to school

INADESU supported children attending the Children Safe Space Bokwaongo as they prepared to return to school. Ms. Leku Sylvie and Ms. Monjoa handed over books and other didactic materials to the children.

![Children at the Children Safe Space receiving books and school materials](/assets/images/news/back-to-school-inadesu1.jpeg)

We wish the children good luck in their studies, and we thank our donors for providing the means to achieve this goal.
