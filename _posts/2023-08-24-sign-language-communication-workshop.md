INADESU partnered with the United Front for Youth Development (UNIFYD) on a project teaching children how to communicate through sign language, with a particular emphasis on children who are disabled.

![Children taking part in the sign language communication workshop](/assets/images/news/sign-language-workshop-2.jpeg)

The initiative aimed to build more inclusive communication skills among children, helping disabled children engage more fully with their peers and communities.
