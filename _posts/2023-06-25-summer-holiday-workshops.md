During the summer holidays, INADESU organized workshops at the Children Safe Space to keep children engaged, active, and learning outside the regular school calendar.

Ms. Leku Sylvie and Mr. Kevin Dzi organized and led the sessions, teaching the children to be responsible, respectful, and hardworking, as well as to value their education.

![Children at the Children Safe Space during the summer holiday workshops](/assets/images/news/summer-holiday-workshops-2.jpg)

The workshops gave children at the Safe Space a structured, supportive environment to build character and good habits during the break from school.
