To commemorate International Women's Day 2024, INADESU organized a workshop to educate young women and girls in digital skills, so that they may take advantage of the various possibilities that have arisen in the modern, digital world.

![Participants at the 2024 Women's Day digital skills workshop](/assets/images/news/womens-day-2024-2.jpeg)

The workshop reflected this year's theme, "Invest in women: Accelerate progress," by equipping participants with practical digital skills to support their future opportunities.
