The entire INADESU team wishes all its friends, supporters, and well-wishers a happy new year 2024. We would also like to thank you all for the support you have been showing us, enabling us to reach out to and support many children in the Buea municipality to build skills in entrepreneurship, languages, public speaking, reading, and math.

Many thanks also go to the University of Lausanne, in particular, for granting us the 2023 CGIU seed funding. This support enabled us to secure a better facility for holding activities at the Children Safe Space (CFS), as well as to acquire some equipment for the safe space.
