One of the INADESU volunteers taught children how to make local sweets as part of an ongoing effort to build practical, income-generating skills from an early age.

![Children making local sweets during the entrepreneurial skills workshop](/assets/images/news/entrepreneurial-skills-workshop-2.jpeg)

After attending this workshop, many children were able to make sweets at home with their parents' help. This kind of hands-on learning enables children to grow up with an entrepreneurial mindset.
