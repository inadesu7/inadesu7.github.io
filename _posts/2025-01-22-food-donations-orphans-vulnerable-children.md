In the past, we often organized visits to give supplies to children in orphanages, and this has always been successful. We, however, realized that in the Buea municipality, and especially in the Bokwaongo, Bova, Molyko, Likoko, Muea, Soppo, and Bokwai neighborhoods, there are many vulnerable children and orphans who live at home with their guardians.

We decided to reach out to over 50 houses with vulnerable children and orphans to donate food supplies in these neighborhoods and many others in Buea.

![INADESU volunteers delivering food donations to a family in Buea](/assets/images/news/food-donations-2025-2.jpeg)

Many thanks to our president, Ms. Sylvie Leku, and other volunteers for coordinating this exercise. Together, let's help children grow healthier and stronger and attain a good education.
