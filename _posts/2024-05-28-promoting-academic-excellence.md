We visited one of the Bokwaongo community's private primary schools. We handed out prizes to children who excelled in their education during the academic year 2023/2024.

![Children receiving academic excellence prizes at the Bokwaongo primary school](/assets/images/news/academic-excellence-2024-2.jpeg)

Recognizing children's hard work and achievement encourages them to keep striving for excellence in their studies.
